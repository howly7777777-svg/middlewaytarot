Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 25, 2026

Thank you for choosing to use "Middle Way Tarot" (hereinafter referred to as "the Application"). We highly value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information.

1. Data Collection and Use

The Application is designed around strict privacy-first and minimalist principles:

  • Local Storage: All of your card draw records, Two-Truths reflection journal entries, and meditation configurations are saved locally on your iOS device.
  • No Server Uploads: We do not transmit or store your journal entries, card draw histories, or personal notes to any external servers. Your spiritual reflections remain completely private.
  • Push Notifications: When you enable daily reminder settings, we trigger them locally using iOS's native notification center (UNUserNotificationCenter). This process is fully offline and does not harvest any identifier data.

We do not sell, trade, or share any personal data with third parties simply because we do not collect or possess any personal data.

The Application offers premium VIP subscription tiers. All payments and subscriptions are processed directly through Apple's App Store purchasing system (StoreKit 2). We do not collect, process, or store credit cards, billing addresses, or transaction histories; all payment processes are securely managed by Apple.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
